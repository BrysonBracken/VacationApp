Mobile Vacation Management App

The Mobile Vacation Management App is an android application designed to help people organize and manage their vacation details all in one location.

Users can create, view, update, and delete vacation and excursion details for any number of vacations. The application features reminders for vacation and excursion dates, generation of vacation reports, and the ability to share vacation details. The vacation stores all data directly and securely on your device.
